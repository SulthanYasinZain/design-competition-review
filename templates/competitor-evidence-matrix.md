## Evidence labels

- `Verified available` — directly supported by a current source.
- `Partial/different workflow` — related capability exists but differs in scope or user flow.
- `Not identified in reviewed public materials` — not found in the sources reviewed; does not prove absence.
- `Unknown` — insufficient evidence.
- `Not applicable` — not relevant to that competitor.

## Red-team questions

- Could a competitor already provide this through a separate service or program?
- Are we comparing equivalent products and user roles?
- Are the sources current?
- Are feature names masking different levels of service?
- Is our differentiation meaningful to users or merely internal to the team?
- Is the feature feasible and testable?
