## Rules

- Preserve numerator and denominator.
- Record question wording where a percentage is involved.
- Record publication year and data-collection year separately.
- Do not record a conclusion as though it were a raw observation.
- Use `NOT DOCUMENTED` where details cannot be found.
- Keep contradictory evidence rather than deleting it.
- Identify claims that need fact-checking before pitch or submission.

## Evidence confidence guide

- **High:** direct, relevant, methodologically clear, and independently inspectable.
- **Medium:** useful but limited by sample, method, date, context, or incomplete documentation.
- **Low:** anecdotal, secondary, vague, old, or only partially relevant.
- **Unverified:** source cannot be checked or details are missing.
