## 5. SUS calculation check (if used)

For each respondent:

- odd-numbered item contribution = response − 1;
- even-numbered item contribution = 5 − response;
- sum all adjusted item contributions;
- multiply sum by 2.5;
- average valid respondent scores.

Record missing responses and handling. Preserve a calculation sheet or anonymized data extract.
